Testing: gingham weave at smaller scales

5 variations · cream #FFFAEE + dusty blue #B7C9CB · original was a 10px cell

As the cell shrinks the check stops reading as a pattern and starts reading as paper texture — which is why none of these need a cream halo behind the type the way the 10px version did. Contrast is tuned per scale: smaller cells need a touch more of it or they vanish, but too much and a fine check will shimmer when the page scrolls.

S1 · 3px micro-weave

Smallest that still reads as woven. At this scale it stops being a pattern and becomes a paper texture — you notice it as warmth, not as check. Needs the contrast up to survive.
